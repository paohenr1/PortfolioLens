CSV format

Use this exact header, then one row per holding:

ticker,exchange_mic,quantity,currency

Enter the listing's ticker and exchange MIC, positive quantity (up to six decimals), and USD. Duplicate ticker/MIC pairs are rejected. A header-only file is allowed for a positive cash-only balance.

Supported MICs: XNAS, XNYS, ARCX, BATS, IEXG, XASE, XNMS, XNCM, XNGS, EDGX, EDGA. Broker exports must be reshaped to this format.
