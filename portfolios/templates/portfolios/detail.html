About these figures

Recorded cash, cost basis and realized sale gains come from your transaction journal using FIFO. They are not current market value. Total value adds cash to holdings valued on the selected closing date. Deposits and withdrawals are separated from investment returns.

Only recorded dividends and splits are included. Missing closing prices, including weekends and holidays, are not filled. Updates use at most eight price requests; closing valuation alone supports five instruments. No automatic refresh is used. Entries do not place orders.
