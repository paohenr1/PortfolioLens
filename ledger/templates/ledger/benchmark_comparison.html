The benchmark links provider-adjusted close ratios across exactly the portfolio's sessions, including weekends and holidays between sessions. Portfolio returns include recorded dividends and fees and remove external funding. The benchmark has no portfolio funding or trading fees.

Adjusted prices are a provider-supplied proxy: adjustment methodology and index tracking have not been verified. This is not an audited total-return index. The return difference is in percentage points, not alpha or a relative wealth ratio. Percentages are rounded only for display.
