How partial-sale cents are allocated

Each lot's cumulative sold cost is its original cost multiplied by the fraction of its original shares sold, rounded half up to USD cents. A sale receives the increase in that cumulative cost. The remainder stays with the open lot, so closing it allocates its full cost exactly. Very small remaining lots may have zero cent-denominated basis.
