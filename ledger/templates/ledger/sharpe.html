The entered annual simple rate is divided by 252 for each session return. Excess return is the portfolio's simple daily return minus that constant per-session rate. Daily Sharpe is mean excess return divided by its sample standard deviation (n−1); annualized Sharpe scales the unrounded ratio by √252.

Requires twenty complete returns and nonzero sample volatility. Ratios have no percent sign. The rate is your assumption, not a retrieved Treasury yield; this session convention does not accrue extra calendar-day interest over weekends or holidays. Annualization assumes stable return behavior without serial correlation. These describe the observed window, not a forecast. Display rounding does not enter calculations, and no additional prices are requested.
