Choose two completed U.S. equity session dates from 2000, covering at most 90 calendar days. The opening close is the baseline; its transactions are already included. Each subsequent row measures the return from the previous session close, including over weekends and holidays.

After the opening date, deposits and withdrawals must occur at end of day after all other activity. Activity on non-session dates is unsupported. Dividends and fees remain in returns; recorded shares must match raw prices and include all applicable splits.

Supports two instruments held anywhere in the period, including positions sold before the end, and at most 16 opening, closing and cash-flow boundary dates. Historical price loads use at most eight requests. Cash-only series request no prices. Loads require confirmation; no automatic refresh or saved prices.

Sessions use the exchange-calendars NYSE regular-session calendar, including early closes and known exceptional closures, for supported U.S. equity venues. Missing, incomplete or unconfirmed prices stay unavailable. No earlier-close substitution or adjusted-price fallback.

Calculations use the corrected journal known now and may restate past results. They depend on complete entries and confirmed flow timing. Risk statistics describe the selected observed window. Optional Sharpe uses your confirmed risk-free assumption.

An optional benchmark adds at most one reference request and one price request. Its adjusted-price return uses the same session dates. Find the provider instrument ID through instrument search; no default benchmark is assumed.

Daily volatility is the sample standard deviation of unrounded simple session returns, with n−1 in the denominator and at least two returns. Annualized volatility scales that variance by 252 and requires twenty returns. This is a convention, not the observed number of sessions in a year or a return forecast.

Drawdown tracks the compounded return after removing external cash flows. Maximum drawdown is the largest percentage loss from a preceding peak in this window, including the opening baseline. Deposits and withdrawals do not create losses or recoveries. The first deepest episode is retained; repeated equal highs use the most recent peak.

All risk results require a complete daily series. Short samples can be unstable. Values display four decimal places; calculations use unrounded factors. These are observed close-to-close results, not intraday or lifetime portfolio risk. Calculating them requests no additional prices.
