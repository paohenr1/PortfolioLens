Opening and closing valuations use confirmed raw daily closes. The opening date is a baseline; activity on that date is already included. The measured period starts after that close and ends at the selected closing date.

Only deposits and withdrawals are external flows. After the opening date, these must occur at end of day after all other activity. The journal order must agree with this timing. Intraday flows are unsupported; daily prices cannot value them accurately.

Loads require confirmation and use at most eight price requests, with at most five holdings on any valuation date and 16 boundary dates. No automatic refresh, adjusted-price fallback, earlier-close substitution, or saved prices.

Recorded dividends and fees remain part of performance. Shares include recorded splits. This depends on complete transactions/actions and confirmed cash-flow timing; it is not broker reconciliation. No annualized return, money-weighted return, benchmark or risk ratio is inferred. Daily session returns have a separate bounded loading action.
