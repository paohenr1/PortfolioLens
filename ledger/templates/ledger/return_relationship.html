Requires at least twenty complete matched session returns. Correlation measures how daily returns move together, from −1 to +1. Beta is the fitted daily-return slope with an intercept against this selected benchmark; 1 means equal sensitivity, and a negative value means opposite direction. Both use unrounded simple returns and display four decimal places without percent signs.

Zero benchmark variance makes both undefined; a constant portfolio has undefined correlation and zero beta against a varying benchmark. These describe this observed window and proxy basis, not future performance or a broad-market beta. Calculating them requests no additional prices.
