Daily return = current selected close / preceding session's selected close − 1. Volatility is the sample standard deviation (n − 1 denominator); annualized volatility multiplies it by √252. At least two returns are needed for daily volatility and twenty for annualization. The 252-session scaling is an assumption, not a forecast.

Drawdown = selected close / highest preceding or current close − 1. Maximum drawdown is the largest decline, displayed as a positive loss percentage. The opening close supplies the initial peak; earlier history is excluded. The first deepest episode is retained, with the most recent equal peak and first subsequent recovery. Daily returns and drawdowns below are signed percentages. Results are rounded half up to four places.

Corporate actions can create apparent losses and volatility.{% endif %} These describe stock prices, with no portfolio holdings, cash flows, fees, tax or currency conversion. Currency metadata may be unavailable; no currency is assumed. No portfolio total return or current freshness guarantee is implied.
